A rule turns one kind of event into notifications: who is told (resolved on the server, only people who may open the item), through which channels, with which text, and whether people can switch it off. When several rules apply to the same person, their channels combine and the rule with the lowest precedence number decides the text, priority and policy. Rules are edited through the API; disabling one here stops it applying.
